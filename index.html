Introduction 01

Welcome to WebSync for WA, developed by Raza's Dev Studio. This Privacy Policy describes our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure when you use our Android application.

WebSync for WA is a productivity companion app that brings WhatsApp Web, QR tools, status saving, file management, and more to your Android device — all while keeping your data private and secure.

Our Core Commitment: Your personal conversations, contacts, and messages are never accessed, stored, or transmitted by us. Your data stays on your device.

Information We Collect 02

We collect very minimal information, and most of it never leaves your device:

  • App Preferences — Theme selection (light/dark/system), notification settings, and other in-app preferences. Stored locally on your device only.
  • WebView Session Data — Cookies and local storage generated by WhatsApp Web. Stored on-device, inside the WebView sandbox. We cannot read this data.
  • Downloaded Files — Files you choose to download via WhatsApp Web are saved to your device's standard Downloads folder. We do not access or copy these files.
  • Saved Statuses — When you save a WhatsApp status using the Status Saver feature, the file is copied to a folder you select on your own device storage.
  • Ad Interaction Data — Google AdMob may collect anonymized interaction data (impressions, clicks) as described in their own privacy policy.
  • Crash Reports — Anonymous crash diagnostics may be collected by the Android platform to help us improve stability.

What We Do NOT Collect 03

We want to be crystal clear about what we never collect:

  • Your WhatsApp messages, chats, or conversations
  • Your phone number, name, or WhatsApp contacts
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Photos, videos, or audio from your device gallery (except files you explicitly share)
  • Your email address or any personal identifiers
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Call logs or SMS messages
  • Browsing history outside the app
  • Financial or payment information
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party, under any circumstances.

Permissions Explained 04

WebSync for WA requests only the permissions necessary to provide its features. Here is exactly why each permission is needed:

INTERNET
Internet Access Required to load WhatsApp Web in the built-in browser, display advertisements, and check for app updates.
CAMERA
Camera Used for scanning QR codes (QR Scanner feature) and for camera-based file uploads inside WhatsApp Web. Only active when you trigger these features.
STORAGE
Read / Write External Storage Needed to save downloaded files from WhatsApp Web, save WhatsApp statuses via Status Saver, and allow File Manager to browse your files. Only accessed when you take an action that requires it.
NOTIFICATIONS
Post Notifications Used to display download progress notifications when files are being downloaded. Optional — you can deny this without losing core functionality.
MANAGE FILES
Manage External Storage (Android 11+) Required on Android 11 and above to access the WhatsApp status folder for the Status Saver feature. Without this permission, statuses cannot be read or saved.
NETWORK STATE
Access Network State Allows the app to detect your network connectivity status and show an appropriate error if there is no internet connection.
The app does not use Accessibility Services, Device Admin privileges, or any system-level permissions beyond what is listed above.

WebView, Cookies & WhatsApp Web Session 05

The WhatsApp Web feature embeds a full web browser (WebView) inside the app. Here is how this data is handled:

  • Cookies — WhatsApp Web stores a session cookie on your device so you stay logged in. This cookie stays entirely within the app's WebView sandbox on your device.
  • Local Storage — WhatsApp Web uses browser local storage to maintain your session. This data is on your device only, in a restricted sandboxed location.
  • Session Persistence — The app keeps you logged in across app restarts by preserving the WebView session (just like a browser does). You can clear this at any time via Settings → Clear WhatsApp Session.
  • We Cannot Read Your Chats — We have no technical ability to intercept or read the contents of your WhatsApp conversations. The WebView renders web content like a browser.
  • External Links — Links clicked inside WhatsApp Web that point outside of WhatsApp are opened in your device's default browser, not stored or tracked by us.

Advertising (Google AdMob) 06

WebSync for WA uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Ads help us keep the app free for everyone. Here is what you should know:

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID) to display relevant ads.
  • We display a Google UMP (User Messaging Platform) consent form as required by Google Play policies, so you can manage your ad preferences.
  • Ads are displayed as App Open Ads, Interstitial Ads, and Rewarded Ads. There are no Banner ads.
  • We request family-safe ad inventory from AdMob. No adult, explicit, sexual, dating, or inappropriate ads are intentionally served.
  • You can manage or revoke your ad consent at any time via Settings → Manage Ad Preferences.
For full details on how Google AdMob collects and uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy 07

WebSync for WA is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy.

  • The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • Only family-friendly advertisements are requested through our AdMob configuration.
  • The app does not contain adult content, violence, or material unsuitable for a general audience.
  • WhatsApp Web itself requires users to be of the age required by WhatsApp's Terms of Service in their country. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children's use of the app.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at the email address below so we can take appropriate action.

Data Security 09

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • All data stored by the app remains within the Android app sandbox, inaccessible to other apps.
  • WebView session data is stored in a restricted, isolated WebView partition.
  • The app does not transmit your personal data to any external servers operated by us.
  • Shared Preferences and local databases are protected by Android's built-in file system security.

While we implement industry-standard safeguards, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will notify users promptly in the event of any breach that could affect them.

Data Retention 10

Since the majority of data generated by WebSync for WA lives only on your device, you are in control of retention:

  • App preferences are retained until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
  • WebView session cookies are retained until you use "Clear WhatsApp Session" in Settings or clear the app's storage via Android Settings.
  • Downloaded files and saved statuses are files on your device — you can delete them at any time using the File Manager or your device's gallery.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored app data automatically.

Your Rights 11

You have the following rights regarding your data and the app:

  • Right to Access — You can view all data stored by the app directly on your device (app settings, saved files).
  • Right to Delete — You can delete all app data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → WebSync for WA → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Right to Opt Out of Ads — Manage your ad consent at any time via Settings → Manage Ad Preferences.
  • Right to Clear Session — Remove your WhatsApp Web session at any time via Settings → Clear WhatsApp Session.
  • Opt Out of Personalized Ads — You can opt out of personalized ads via your Android device's Advertising settings.
